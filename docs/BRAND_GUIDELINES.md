# MiniBites Brand Guidelines

## Final direction

- Brand: **MiniBites**
- Product: **MiniBites Studio**
- Primary tagline: **Create tiny food. Tell big stories.**
- Arabic tagline: **أكلات صغيرة. قصص كبيرة.**
- Secondary tagline: **I have an idea. MiniBites turns it into content.**
- One-sentence pitch: MiniBites Studio turns a food idea into a coherent miniature vertical video, ready to review and publish.
- 30-word description: A creator-first studio that plans, generates, reviews and packages miniature food videos for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts—without requiring knowledge of AI models or video pipelines.

## Slogan evaluation

| Option | Memorability | Clarity | Creator appeal | Food relevance | Global use |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Create tiny food. Tell big stories. | 10 | 9 | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Tiny dishes. Big content. | 8 | 10 | 9 | 9 | 10 |
| Small bites. Big imagination. | 8 | 8 | 9 | 9 | 10 |
| Cook small. Create big. | 9 | 8 | 9 | 9 | 9 |
| Tiny food. Viral stories. | 8 | 8 | 8 | 10 | 9 |

The recommended line avoids promising virality and gives the product an emotional creator narrative.

## Personality and voice

Playful, premium, creative, food-led, modern, friendly, colorful and honest. Use simple creator vocabulary: Idea, Style, Shots, Generate, Review, Publish and Library. Technical provider/job details belong in Advanced or Operations.

## Color system

- Tomato/coral `#ff6b5f`: primary creator action and food energy.
- Fresh green `#29b886`: success and ready states.
- Creative violet `#7657ff`: planning and brand distinction.
- Orange/gold `#f3b52f`: review and attention.
- Sky `#66d7f0`: generation activity.
- Cream `#fffaf5`: warm canvas.
- Charcoal `#18211d`: high-contrast text.

Status use: Planning violet; Generating blue; Ready green; Review amber; Failed red; Published teal. Avoid generic AI-purple gradients as the entire identity.

## Typography

Manrope for display and product headings; DM Sans for interface and body copy; system Arabic sans fallback until a verified Arabic webfont is bundled locally.

## Logo direction

Use a simple plate/play symbol with an `MB` monogram. It must remain legible at favicon size, in one color, as a watermark, and inside circular social avatars. Avoid robots, sparkles as the main symbol, and complex illustrations.
